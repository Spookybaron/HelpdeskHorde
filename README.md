# Helpdesk Horde

A survivors-style browser game. You are the only IT technician in the building, it is 09:00, and everyone is walking towards you with a problem. Fire emails at them, book training courses as you level up, and keep your hair until 17:00.

To play, open `index.html` in a browser. It is also on my site under Games.

## How to play

- Drag anywhere to move, or use WASD / the arrow keys.
- Emails fire on their own at the nearest person.
- Collect closed tickets to level up and book a course.
- If people reach you, your stress rises. At 100% the hair goes.
- Meeting invites land where you stand. Walk out of them.

## Files

- `index.html` — the game.
- `trailer.js` — the intro trailer that plays on a first visit.
- `voices.js` — the recorded voice clips for every saying, made with ElevenLabs.

There is nothing to build or install.
